# Friction Log

Record genuine developer friction encountered while building the ACT III project. This is evidence, not a complaint list.

## Entry format

### YYYY-MM-DD — Short title

**Surface:** AMD / ROCm / event platform / UCII integration / deployment / other

**Task:** What we were trying to accomplish.

**Observed friction:** Exact behavior or obstacle.

**Evidence:** Error, documentation ambiguity, reproducible steps, screenshot/reference, or measured impact. Do not record secrets.

**Impact:** Time lost, implementation compromise, reliability risk, judging/demo risk, or none.

**Workaround:** What resolved or mitigated it.

**Suggested improvement:** Specific product/documentation/tooling improvement.

**Status:** OPEN / MITIGATED / RESOLVED / INFORMATIONAL

---

## Principles

- Record facts as they happen; do not reconstruct flattering friction after the build.
- Separate event-platform friction from AMD technical friction.
- Never include credentials, tokens, private keys, recovery material, or reusable authorization/payment proofs.
- Preserve failures that later become resolved; historical friction remains useful evidence.
